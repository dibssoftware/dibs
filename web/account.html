Sign in to dibs

Your account remembers your Macs, so any browser you sign in with opens all of them, encrypted end to end.

The relay keeps only a keyed hash of your address, and uses it only to send the code.

Check your email

We sent a 6-digit code.

The email also has a link, for signing in on another device.

Add a passkey

Next time, sign in with Touch ID, Face ID or your password manager, no email needed. A passkey that holds your account key opens your Macs at once in any new browser.

Skip it? Without a passkey, every new browser needs an email code and one of your Macs to let it in.

One more step

You're signed in, but this browser doesn't have your account's key yet. One of your Macs can give it: ask here, then run the command this page shows on that Mac.

Signing in with a passkey that holds the key skips this.
