Sign in to dibs

Your account remembers your Macs, so any browser you sign in with opens all of them. What you see stays encrypted between this browser and your Macs.

The relay keeps only a keyed hash of your address, and uses the address only to send the link.

One more step

You're signed in, but this browser doesn't have your account's key yet. One of your Macs can give it: ask here, then run the command this page shows on that Mac.

Signing in with a passkey that holds the key skips this.
